## 1. Introduction

Multimodal web agents that combine browser screenshots with DOM-derived text act through representations (the scope this paper validates; see §8.1 for the bounded-comparison disclosure of the agent-scaffolding class — text-only or coordinate-only agents fall outside this scope, B-1281 /stress A2.6b P1-9-AC 2026-05-18). A browser state can be serialized as a DOM or Accessibility Tree, shown as a screenshot, or annotated with Set-of-Mark (SoM) labels that connect visible regions to discrete element IDs. Existing benchmarks and agents treat these as different observation modes: WebArena and Mind2Web popularized DOM-derived text for realistic web tasks, while VisualWebArena and SeeAct introduced visually grounded settings where screenshots and action grounding become central \citep{zhou2024webarena,deng2023mind2web,koh2024visualwebarena,zheng2024seeact}. Set-of-Mark prompting was designed for this multimodal setting: a marked image is paired with a textual legend so the model can refer to visual objects by number \citep{yang2023som}. Later multimodal-agent systems, including SeeAct and Magma, further explored marked-screenshot and omni-modal action-grounding paradigms rather than treating mark text as an isolated scientific variable \citep{zheng2024seeact,yang2025magma}. These are important precedents. We therefore do not claim to be first to deploy text-only, marked, or SoM-style observations. Our claim is about controlled characterization: isolating what changes when the annotated image is skipped while the text payload and prompt family are varied under the same task, model, and evaluation protocol.

This paper questions that bundling assumption as an experimental object. We characterize the **phantom routing space**: the set of agent configurations **formed by varying text-payload format (AXTree ↔ `[SOM_MARKS]`) and prompt family (DOM-prompt ↔ SoM-prompt) from the DOM baseline**, all on the "skip annotated image" boundary that removes the **marked page screenshot**.[^image-scope] [^phantom-boundary-formulation] [^scaffolding-class-scope] DOM is the **origin baseline** (axis-0 in the 2×2 ablation diamond); the three perturbation arms are **P-text** (text-axis alone: `[SOM_MARKS]` under DOM-prompt), **P-prompt** (prompt-axis alone: SoM-prompt over AXTree), and **P-SoM** (compound: `[SOM_MARKS]` under SoM-prompt). P-SoM is the deployment-relevant representative — the agent receives the SoM prompt and the `[SOM_MARKS]` textual element list, but no marked screenshot. At the start of this project, P-SoM looked like a broken ablation. The natural expectation was that removing the marked screenshot would collapse SoM into either a weak DOM surrogate or a nonsensical configuration: the prompt still suggests visual marks, but the visual substrate is absent. The archived B0 runs reject that expectation. Phantom-SoM solves tasks that DOM, full SoM, and Vision all miss, and on B0 reddit it is comparable to full SoM within sampling error by VWA-Success (**13.81% vs 10.48%, archive denominator N=210** — these archive percentages predate the §139.8 N/A-exclusion lock; the scored-set recomputation at N=205 lands with Phase 1a; the gap is within 2σ under the run-to-run variability we observe in same-condition repeats), while avoiding the per-step marked-screenshot encoding cost. On classifieds, full SoM remains clearly stronger (**21.37% vs Phantom-SoM 14.53%, archive denominator N=234**; scored-set N=224 recomputation pending likewise), the expected sanity check when marked screenshots carry real visual information.

The paper's empirical claims are scoped to this class. See §8.1 cross-family bounded-comparison stack for the parallel multi-baseline disclosure.

**Phase 1a outcome: the preregistered H1 superiority gate does not pass, and the paper's claim rests on H3 instead.** Over the full 6-mode universe and all six preregistered (site, model) cells, P-SoM's pooled drop-one effect is **θ_FE = 0.7897 pp** (paired-bootstrap pooled median 0.7490 pp, 95% CI [0.2858, 1.4471]), tested one-sided against the preregistered margin of δ = 1.0 pp: **p = 0.807, H1 FAIL**. The per-cell effects are consistently small rather than noisily dispersed, so additional cells would not move the pool across the margin. Following the preregistered decision rule this places the paper at **framing R5** with the **C'-S structural pivot**, and we report it as the primary Phase 1a result rather than as a caveat.

What survives, and what the paper is now built on, is the **structural decomposition H3**. Both preregistered axes clear their gates by four to six orders of magnitude in p: **axis-1 θ_FE = 1.3528 pp (p = 1.19 × 10⁻⁵)** and **axis-2 θ_FE = 2.0877 pp (p = 7.52 × 10⁻⁷)**, each with five of six cells passing individually. The phantom space is therefore established as a *structured* region, decomposable along text-format and prompt-family axes, rather than as a region containing one superior deployment arm. Complementarity remains measurable at the task level: P-SoM uniquely solves **6 classifieds and 3 reddit tasks** that no other of the six modes solves.

Our third contribution is a **representation-axis cost-aware router** that exploits the phantom routing space. **Unlike model-routing systems (FrugalGPT \citep{chen2023frugalgpt}, RouteLLM \citep{ong2025routellm}, and the uncertainty-decomposition framework of \citep{peale2026flexibleRouting}) whose arms differ in *model* under fixed input representation, the phantom routing space holds the model fixed and varies the *representation*; the deployment cost asymmetry comes from representation choice rather than from model scale.** The behavioural two-knob account predicts a routing signal at the *task* level: tasks whose intrinsic structure rewards quick decision-and-commit behaviour (forum-style lookup, list-traversal queries) should benefit from the flat `[SOM_MARKS]` text representation, while tasks rewarding sustained exploration (multi-step navigation, comparison shopping) should benefit from the hierarchical AXTree representation (**to be tested on VWA cls/red task pools at the prereg-locked 6-cell scope — archive-cell evidence only until Phase 1a lands; cross-pool task-structure generalization remains future work — see §8 future-work cube-concept generalizability paragraph**, B-1293 /stress A2.6b P1-15-A* 2026-05-18 — the task-structure-based prediction is a super-population claim about task-feature → mode mapping but is empirically supported only by the finite 6-cell task pool).
