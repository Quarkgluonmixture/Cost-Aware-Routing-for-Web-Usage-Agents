## 1. Introduction

Web agents act through representations. A browser state can be serialized as a DOM or Accessibility Tree, shown as a screenshot, or annotated with Set-of-Mark (SoM) labels that connect visible regions to discrete element IDs. Existing benchmarks and agents treat these as different observation modes: WebArena and Mind2Web popularized DOM-derived text for realistic web tasks, while VisualWebArena and SeeAct introduced visually grounded settings where screenshots and action grounding become central \citep{zhou2024webarena,deng2023mind2web,koh2024visualwebarena,zheng2024seeact}. Set-of-Mark prompting was designed for this multimodal setting: a marked image is paired with a textual legend so the model can refer to visual objects by number \citep{yang2023som}. Later multimodal-agent systems, including SeeAct and Magma, further explored marked-screenshot and omni-modal action-grounding paradigms rather than treating mark text as an isolated scientific variable \citep{zheng2024seeact,yang2025magma}. These are important precedents. We therefore do not claim to be first to deploy text-only, marked, or SoM-style observations. Our claim is about controlled characterization: isolating what changes when the annotated image is skipped while the text payload and prompt family are varied under the same task, model, and evaluation protocol.

This paper questions that bundling assumption as an experimental object. We characterize the **phantom routing space**: the set of agent configurations **formed by varying text-payload format (AXTree ↔ `[SOM_MARKS]`) and prompt family (DOM-prompt ↔ SoM-prompt) from the DOM baseline**, all on the "skip annotated image" boundary that removes the **marked page screenshot**.[^image-scope] [^phantom-boundary-formulation] DOM is the **origin baseline** (axis-0 in the 2×2 ablation diamond); the three perturbation arms are **P-text** (text-axis alone: `[SOM_MARKS]` under DOM-prompt), **P-prompt** (prompt-axis alone: SoM-prompt over AXTree), and **P-SoM** (compound: `[SOM_MARKS]` under SoM-prompt). P-SoM is the deployment-relevant representative — the agent receives the SoM prompt and the `[SOM_MARKS]` textual element list, but no marked screenshot. At the start of this project, P-SoM looked like a broken ablation. The natural expectation was that removing the marked screenshot would collapse SoM into either a weak DOM surrogate or a nonsensical configuration: the prompt still suggests visual marks, but the visual substrate is absent. The data reject that expectation. Phantom-SoM solves tasks that DOM, full SoM, and Vision all miss, and on B0 reddit it matches or modestly exceeds full SoM by VWA-Success (N/A excluded) (**13.81% vs 10.48%, N=210**; the gap is within 2σ under the run-to-run variability we observe in same-condition repeats), while avoiding the per-step marked-screenshot encoding cost. On classifieds, full SoM remains clearly stronger (**21.37% vs Phantom-SoM 14.53%, N=234**), the expected sanity check when marked screenshots carry real visual information.

[^phantom-boundary-formulation]: **Boundary formulation: axis-based vs 4-corner.** /stress A2.1 P0-3-AC* (2026-05-17, Claude+gemini overlap) correction. Two equivalent formulations of the phantom-space boundary appear in earlier drafts: (i) an **axis-based** formulation in which the phantom space is the set of perturbation arms {P-text, P-prompt, P-SoM} formed by varying text-payload format and/or prompt family from the DOM baseline along the 2×2 ablation diamond on the image-off boundary; and (ii) an architectural **4-corner** formulation in `paper_planning §2` Zoom 1 in which the phantom space includes DOM as the image-off cube quadrant origin (4 corners total). The two formulations describe the same set of configurations but differ in whether DOM is a phantom-space *member* or a phantom-space *anchor*. Our first contribution is a controlled scientific evaluation of this phantom boundary. Across completed B0 VisualWebArena classifieds and reddit runs, we compare DOM, full SoM, Vision, and Phantom-SoM on the same task sets (**N=234 classifieds; N=210 reddit; same-task VWA-Success (N/A excluded)**) and use the P-text/P-prompt controls to test whether the effect collapses to one prompt trick or one text-format swap. Phantom-SoM is not the best single arm on every site, and we do not claim that it replaces full SoM. Its value is complementarity. The principal hero metric is the **drop-one oracle**, not the single-mode SR difference. The numbers reported here are from the **archive 4-mode universe** {DOM, SoM, Vision, P-SoM}: Phantom-SoM contributes **3.33 percentage points** of incremental oracle value on reddit with a **task-resampling 95% two-sided CI of [+0.95, +6.19]** (per-task paired bootstrap, B=1000 resamples; the prereg-locked H1 superiority gate is a **one-sided** test against δ=1.0pp at α=0.05, reported as a separate per-cell p-value column in §4 — the 95% two-sided CI shown here is the descriptive-uncertainty channel, not the gate test statistic — B-1008 /stress A2.4a P1-16-C*), comparable to full SoM at +1.90pp [+0.48, +3.81], and **2.56 percentage points** on classifieds with CI [+0.85, +4.70].[^hero-estimand-scope] [^null-framing] Cross-mode Jaccard similarity falls in the **0.29-0.49 range** against other arms; this is **3-5× higher than the random-independent baseline** (E[J] ≈ 0.06-0.10 given the observed marginal pass rates), indicating substantial pass-correlation across modes. The complementarity claim therefore rests on the **residual unique-pass set** being large enough to drive non-trivial drop-one oracle excess (P-SoM solves on the order of 7 reddit tasks and 6 classifieds tasks that no other mode in the 4-mode archive universe solves; §4 success-pool table), not on Jaccard being absolutely low. The head-to-head reddit single-mode contrast (Phantom-SoM 13.81% vs full SoM 10.48%) gives a marginal CI [-0.95, +7.62] that crosses zero (P(diff>0)=0.914), exactly the "within 2σ" caveat above; we therefore frame the head-to-head SR contrast as competitive parity, and let the **drop-one oracle excess over the fixed-marginal permutation null** (§4.Y) carry the deployment-relevant claim.

**Drop-in property — constructed vs measured.** By construction, phantom-space arms inherit DOM's cost profile and lower per-step latency: the `[SOM_MARKS]` block is produced by a regex pass over the same accessibility-tree text the DOM agent already consumes (interactive elements come pre-numbered as `[N] role 'label'`), so a deployment that can run DOM can run Phantom-SoM by changing what it forwards to the model — no bounding-box pipeline, no per-step marked screenshot, no extra inference modality at every browser step. These are **architectural consequences of the image-off boundary**, not empirical discoveries. On top of this constructed substrate, we **empirically validate two non-trivial signal properties**: (c) routing signal AUROC ≥ baseline (§4.X) and (d) strictly-positive drop-one oracle excess over fixed-marginal permutation null (§4.Y).[^cost-basis-cross-baseline] **The drop-one number is an oracle ceiling, not a realized router gain; Section 6 reports realized lift after router classification overhead and misrouting regret, alongside the oracle bound for context.**

[^null-framing]: **Null hypothesis specification.** /stress A2.1 P0-2-ABC* (2026-05-17, Claude+codex+gemini 3-AI overlap) correction. The task-resampling 95% CI quoted here estimates the **per-task superpopulation variability** of the drop-one oracle functional under the standard task-exchangeability assumption; rejecting `H₀: drop_one = 0` only rules out the trivial null that P-SoM solves zero unique tasks in finite N, which any non-zero unique-pass count would beat. The **paper-grade superiority claim** rests on a separate, stricter null: drop-one oracle excess over a **fixed-marginal permutation reference** (each arm's pass count is held at the observed value, task-mode pass labels are shuffled within marginals across B'=10000 permutations, recomputing drop-one each time). The reported deployment-relevant claim is "observed P-SoM drop-one falls above the 95th percentile of this permutation distribution by Δ pp", not "observed drop-one is significantly greater than zero". The implementation (`scripts/analysis/aggregate_phantom_lift.py --permute-marginal-null`) and per-cell permutation excess values are reported in §4.Y; the strict-positive bootstrap CI is retained in §1 as descriptive evidence of effect direction. Sensitivity to the exchangeability assumption is addressed by stratified bootstrap by task-family / evaluator-type / reference-image-presence (`--stratified-bootstrap`), reported in §4.Y as a robustness column.

The cross-site asymmetry is itself informative: full SoM clearly outperforms Phantom-SoM on classifieds, where visually rich product listings make layout and appearance important, but not on reddit, where post and comment threads are more text-dominated. This is consistent with marked screenshots being most useful when visual grounding is task-critical.

Our second contribution is a behavioural **hypothesis-generation observation** about why the ablation works. A 2-by-2 reddit ablation separates text representation from prompt family: DOM prompt versus SoM prompt, crossed with AXTree versus `[SOM_MARKS]` text. On the **archive verified same-task subset (N=48)**, replacing AXTree with `[SOM_MARKS]` shifts exploration away from DOM-like search loops and toward Phantom-SoM-like quick decisions: the search-loop rate is **22.7% for DOM** but **10.8% for both Phantom-SoM and P-text** — a substantively large behavioural shift on an evaluator-stable metric. The prompt knob signal is **weaker and more provisional**: on the same N=48 archive subset, DOM-prompt arms show a raw-to-adjusted false-positive gap of **6.25 pp (3 N/A false positives)** while Phantom-SoM under the SoM prompt has a gap of **2.08 pp (1 N/A false positive)**, but this is an absolute difference of **2 tasks at N=48** drawn from a post-hoc adjusted-success layer (`compute_adjusted_success`) that §3 retires under the canonical evaluator-level fix (`section3_definition.md:119`). We therefore frame the two-knob view as a **hypothesis to be validated under Phase 1a clean-rerun evaluator-stable metrics** (finish-before-evidence rate, finish step index, empty-answer rate, parse-valid finish rate, judge-triggered success among finish actions): **text representation appears to shape exploration; prompt wording appears to modulate commitment timing**. This is consistent with prior evidence that language models are highly sensitive to prompt format \citep{sclar2024promptformat,mishra2022reframing}, but the multi-step web-agent extension is **archive-grade evidence pending Phase 1a confirmation**. (Mid-layer activation-patching evidence for this two-knob account is deferred to a follow-up mechanism paper; the present paper reports the behavioural claim only.)

Our third contribution is a **representation-axis cost-aware router** that exploits the phantom routing space. **Unlike model-routing systems (FrugalGPT \citep{chen2023frugalgpt}, RouteLLM \citep{ong2025routellm}, and the uncertainty-decomposition framework of \citep{peale2026flexibleRouting}) whose arms differ in *model* under fixed input representation, the phantom routing space holds the model fixed and varies the *representation*; the deployment cost asymmetry comes from representation choice rather than from model scale.** The behavioural two-knob account predicts a routing signal at the *task* level: tasks whose intrinsic structure rewards quick decision-and-commit behaviour (forum-style lookup, list-traversal queries) should benefit from the flat `[SOM_MARKS]` text representation, while tasks rewarding sustained exploration (multi-step navigation, comparison shopping) should benefit from the hierarchical AXTree representation. The phantom arms' **constructed** cost-equivalence with DOM provides the **substrate** for cost-aware representation routing; whether **realized** router lift compensates router classification overhead and misrouting regret is reported in §6 alongside the oracle ceiling for context. Section 6 operationalises this with two complementary routing approaches (paper §1 + §6 prose corrected post /stress A1.10 P0-6/P0-7-C* hallucination catch 2026-05-16): (a) a **rule-based escalation router** that switches mode reactively on dynamic step feedback (unchanged-page streak, action-failure streak, raw DOM-size signal), implementing the v3/v4/v5 single-step `modes[idx+1]` escalation — note that escalation pays the DOM cost first and only adds phantom-arm cost on misroute, so the realized router cost is **DOM + α × phantom-arm** rather than phantom-arm-only; the v6 pareto-cascade variant (`router.py::latch_after_fallback`) is deferred to a follow-up paper, and (b) a **learned classifier** that predicts best-mode-per-task from TF-IDF task-instruction features plus binary task features (presence of reference image, intent keyword regexes, AXTree element count) under 5-fold site-stratified cross-validation, with the classifier's training and per-task inference cost amortised into the §6 realized-lift accounting. The two routers consume disjoint signal sources — the rule-based one observes the running episode, the learned one observes the task statically — and are reported as separate operating points rather than a unified system. We compare both against oracle-ceiling, best-single-mode, and random baselines. Behaviour-pattern routing (route by *mode behaviour* rather than task attribute) and tier-1-oracle / tier-2-leak-free router framings are deferred to a follow-up paper.

We evaluate on VisualWebArena classifieds and reddit with three baselines: **B0** Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B (proxy API, high-capability behavioural anchor), **B1** Qwen3-VL-4B (local, open-weight within-family comparison), and **B2** Gemma3-VL `google/gemma-3-4b-it` (local, **cross-family robustness check at 4B parameter parity with B1 (note: 4B parity is parameter count only, not a strict matched-capability anchor — see model_card and section8 for the bounded-comparison disclosure)**). B0-vs-B1 contrasts a high-capability commercial-proxy anchor against an open-weight local baseline (a joint deployment-class × capability-scale comparison rather than a pure capability ablation — see §8 for the deployment-class asymmetry disclosure stack covering API vs local cost basis, scroll-action vocabulary, tokenizer family, and the now-retired GLM rescue scaffold); B1-vs-B2 contrasts open-weight family-level differences at the same 4B parameter scale (a parameter-parity robustness check across Qwen vs Gemma lineage, not a full matched-capability anchor — see §3 model card + §8 cross-family limitations). **Scroll-action vocabulary execution-layer threat (B-333 + B-902 reframing /stress A2.2 P1-15-C):** B0 emits semantic scroll direction (`up`/`down`) via the proxy API and the agent layer fix-converts to a constant normalized step magnitude (±0.8); B1 and B2 emit free-form `delta: [dx, dy]` predicted per-step by the VLM. On reddit (where comment-thread scroll is task-critical for roughly the share of tasks whose `intent` field references "scroll" / "search" / "comments" / "thread" / "post") the B1/B2 free-form scroll grants a measurable execution-layer advantage over B0's fixed-magnitude semantic scroll; we report this not as a passive "disclosure" but as a bounded execution-layer threat to cross-baseline SR interpretation (§3 + §8 quantify the upper bound). We deliberately do not standardize the action vocabulary post-hoc because doing so would obscure the deployment-relevant comparison (B0 ships through the proxy as-deployed with its tool-schema scroll semantic).
