\subsection*{8.1 Scope \& external-validity bounds}

This study is deliberately narrow: three sites (classifieds, reddit, shopping; Phase 1a workshop scope = classifieds + reddit only with shopping deferred to Phase 1b main paper), one benchmark family (VisualWebArena's three task sites: classifieds, reddit, shopping; WebArena cross-benchmark validation explicit future work per `pre_run/preregistration.md §7` — B-1270 /stress A2.6a P1-12-A 2026-05-18, supersedes prior "WA-mini-style task structure" phrasing that ambiguously implied WA-like task scope within Phase 1a), and three baselines: **B0** Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B via proxy API, **B1** Qwen3-VL-4B local, and **B2** Gemma3-VL `google/gemma-3-4b-it` local (added 2026-05-14 as cross-family robustness check at 4B parameter parity vs B1 (a parameter-parity comparison, not a strict matched-capability anchor — Gemma vs Qwen lineage have different vision encoders, pretraining, and instruction-tuning, so this is a robustness-direction test, not a controlled-capability ceiling)). The blast radius is external validity, not the internal comparison: our claim is that a phantom routing space exists for Qwen-lineage 2-cell within-family verification (B0 235B + B1 4B) + Gemma-lineage 1-cell cross-family direction test (B2 4B) on VWA-style tasks under controlled 3-baseline comparison (B0/B1 within-family across capability scale; B1/B2 cross-family at the same 4B parameter scale (parameter-parity, not matched-capability)) — **with the explicit prereg-locked cross-family claim-tier gate (B-1284 /stress A2.6b P0-3-AC* + user Q1 hybrid decision 2026-05-18, `pre_run/preregistration.md §2.5` step 8): B2 H1 outcome separately gates the R-tier of the cross-family direction claim — B2 fail while Qwen 2 cells pass downgrades R-tier one step + paper reports "Qwen-lineage 2-cell verified; Gemma-lineage 1-cell did not replicate", and does NOT collapse the phantom space construct or trigger R5**. **Phantom space construct scope (B-1280 /stress A2.6b P0-1-AC* 2026-05-18, Claude Mode A + gemini Mode C 2-AI OOB overlap)**: the construct itself presupposes screenshot-based SoM-style scaffolding (text-only LLM agents, coordinate-only agents, and raw-pixel agents do not exhibit this boundary — see §1 footnote `[^scaffolding-class-scope]` for the agent-scaffolding-class disclosure). Cross-architecture generalization beyond Qwen+Gemma, cross-benchmark generalization beyond VWA, AND cross-scaffolding-class generalization beyond SoM-style multimodal web agents all remain outside the paper's empirical claims — not that every VLM, browser benchmark, production site, or non-SoM agent scaffolding will expose the same arm ordering \citep{zhou2024webarena,koh2024visualwebarena,deng2023mind2web,drouin2024workarena}. **N=3 underdetermined-ness (B-1236 /stress A2.4b Chunk β P0-1-AC*, 2026-05-18)**: at three baselines, two orthogonal cross-baseline axes — deployment-tier (\{B0 API\} vs \{B1, B2 local\}) and family (\{B0, B1 Qwen\} vs \{B2 Gemma\}) — cannot be mathematically isolated; any single cross-baseline phantom-property delta is attributable to either axis, an interaction, or both, without a 4th matched-corner baseline (e.g., Qwen-via-API at 4B scale, or Gemma deployed via API). We accordingly scope B0-vs-B1 gaps as deployment-tier differences (not capability-scaling effects in isolation) and B1-vs-B2 gaps as 4B-parity cross-family robustness checks (not family-universality claims), and the §2 R1-R5 framing binds the cross-baseline assertion to qualitative-direction preservation across these lineage differences rather than quantitative replication. Mid-layer mechanism evidence (activation patching, layer probes, logit lens, SAE) is deferred to a follow-up paper per advisor scope-flip 2026-05-14; the present paper rests on the behavioural two-knob account (text representation × prompt family) plus the cost-aware router results. Shopping has weaker intermediate coverage than classifieds/reddit, so site-level generalization is reported cell-by-cell rather than averaged into a universal web-agent claim. This affects Sections 1, 5, and 6 by bounding language about universality and reproducibility: B1 + B2 behavior is byte-reproducible from released artifacts on a self-hosted A100 VWA Docker stack (HF SHAs pinned per `pre_run/locked_versions.md`), while B0 is verifiable from traces and replayable subject to API access; cross-architecture claims beyond Qwen+Gemma, including GPT-4o-family and Llama-family claims, remain outside the paper.

\subsection*{8.2 Construct validity \& evaluator threats}

VWA success labels are imperfect measurements of task completion, especially around `ua_match` GPT-judge drift, the `string_match` `fuzzy_threshold` misnomer, brittle `program_html` selectors, and `finish_wrong_state` episodes. The blast radius is measurement-side: these four evaluator-class threats can flip individual labels or inflate raw success, but they do not redefine the task universe or the mode definitions; full appendix prose is kept in Section 4.X.1--4.X.4 rather than duplicated here. Following the 2026-05-14 §139.8 FP-architecture restructure, the dominant evaluator FP source (the LLM judge scoring an empty prediction as correct in `llm_fuzzy_match` / `llm_ua_match`) is fixed at the VWA evaluator itself (B-91 source-level patch in submodule branch `p79-patches`), and N/A tasks are excluded at task-load (`exclude_na_tasks: true`, 73 N/A tasks across VWA+WA), so raw `success` from the fixed evaluator is the **internal-P79 paper-grade outcome** (`adjusted_success ≡ success`); the prior post-hoc `compute_adjusted_success` filter and 3-variant sensitivity ladder (`na_fp` / `eval_fp` / `visual_fp`) are retired and retained only for pre-§139.8 archive contamination disclosure in Appendix D \citep{lipton2018troubling,neurips2024checklist}. We deliberately do not call this outcome ``canonical'' in a cross-paper sense: the LLM judge is configurable via the `VWA_EVAL_MODEL` environment variable (default `gpt-4o-mini`, differing from upstream VWA's deprecated `gpt-4-1106-preview` --- see Section 3.5 ``LLM-judge model disclosure''), so SR comparisons against the original VWA, WebArena-Verified, or PAE baselines require a judge-model-equivalent rerun before they are paired. This affects Sections 3 and 4: raw `success` is the headline metric for internal P79 paired comparisons (DOM ↔ P-SoM, baseline ↔ phantom within the same cell), the source-level fixes are disclosed transparently, and claims are defensible as representation effects rather than judge artifacts under the (fixed) evaluator at the chosen judge model; cross-paper SR comparisons are scoped to qualitative direction rather than point-estimate equivalence.

\subsection*{8.3 Internal-validity threats: known scaffold bugs}

Several scaffold bugs were real: the `in_viewport_ratio` operator-precedence bug exposed clipped DOM text, early scroll actions suffered direction-convention confusion, and Stage 2B/2C mechanism inputs came from pre-Phase-A archived browser states. The blast radius is bounded because these failures are mode-uniform within the relevant comparisons: the viewport bug affects DOM-derived text and Phantom-SoM's `[SOM_MARKS]` source together; scroll-direction confusion is a trajectory-execution threat rather than an evaluator rule; and Stage 2 uses frozen prompt/screenshot inputs, so Phase-A dispatch bugs affect which step an agent reached, not the model's forward pass on that saved step. This affects cross-mode interpretation in Sections 3--5: we treat environment failures as measurement threats and disclose their blast radius rather than folding them into cognitive claims.

\paragraph{Action-vocabulary cross-baseline asymmetry (B-406 / /stress A1.22 P2-20-C Gemini, 2026-05-17).} Coordinate-format emission rate differs systematically across baselines: empirical archive shows B0 emits pixel-format coordinates 15.6\% of clicks vs B1 35.3\% (a 2.3\(\times\) gap, source \texttt{section3\_definition.md:137}); B2's rate is to-be-determined post-Phase-1a fire. The wrapper accepts both normalized and pixel formats interchangeably via value-based heuristic (\texttt{vwa\_wrapper.py:360-377}), so the execution-layer behaviour is identical, but the model-internal coordinate-format preference is part of each baseline's capability bundle (B0's tool schema permits both; B1/B2 prompted JSON tends to emit pixels more often). Cross-baseline SR claims that depend on click precision in dense UI components should be read with this disclosure in mind --- coordinate format preference is not pure representation effect but a measurement-vocabulary confound we deliberately do not standardize away, because standardizing post-hoc would obscure the deployment-relevant behaviour (B0 ships through the proxy as-deployed with its tool-schema preferences).

\paragraph{Cross-family bounded-comparison structural list (B-916 / B-917 / B-918, /stress A2.2 P1-14/16/17-C* Gemini OOB, 2026-05-17).} The B1-vs-B2 cross-family comparison is bounded by four structural differences orthogonal to the ``phantom routing space'' claim under test, listed here so reviewers can read SR deltas with each factor accounted for: (i) \textbf{Chat template} --- B1 ChatML vs B2 Gemma3 IT template; both encode role boundaries differently and a SoM-style instruction may be tokenized at different positions. (ii) \textbf{Instruction-tuning data mixture} --- Qwen3 and Gemma3 release notes disclose IT mixture composition partially; whether either lineage's IT data includes web-agent-style trajectories with numbered \texttt{[N]} references is opaque. (iii) \textbf{Tokenizer family} --- Qwen3 BPE (151,936 vocab) vs Gemma3 SentencePiece (262,144 vocab) differ in compression rate for the \texttt{[N] role 'label'} flat-text payload; cross-baseline absolute \texttt{input\_text\_tokens} subtraction is therefore not the apples-to-apples metric (use within-baseline $\Delta$tokens(mode) / tokens(DOM) instead --- see \texttt{section3\_definition.md \S 3.5.1} \emph{per-mode input-token decomposition} and tokenizer-asymmetry paragraphs). (iv) \textbf{Vision encoder} --- B1 native Qwen-family dynamic-resolution ViT vs B2 SigLIP fixed 896$\times$896 with 256-tokens-per-image budget; on visually rich tasks (classifieds product detail) a B2-vs-B1 SR delta cannot be attributed solely to ``LLM reasoning differences'' without disentangling perception. Combined implication: B1-vs-B2 is a \textbf{4B parameter-parity robustness check}, not a controlled-capability ceiling. The paper hero claims (drop-one oracle, cost $\approx$ DOM, 4-fold drop-in property) hold \emph{within each baseline} and the cross-baseline assertion is that the qualitative direction is preserved across the (chat-template $\times$ IT-data $\times$ tokenizer $\times$ vision-encoder) lineage difference, not that B2 quantitatively replicates B1. \textbf{B2 commitment without fallback path (B-1020 /stress A2.4a P1-18-C*, 2026-05-18)}: the prereg-locked 6-cell FE pool includes B2 (Gemma3-VL) on equal footing with B0 + B1; we deliberately do \emph{not} pre-register a B2-DOM-baseline-capability threshold or fallback to k=4 Qwen-only main pool. The mechanism trust rests on three independent literature anchors validated cross-family — Mirage Effect (Asadi et al.\ 2026 arXiv:2603.21687) replicated across GPT-4V/4o + Claude 3 + Gemini 1.5 Pro + Qwen-VL/LLaVA/InternVL, Scaffold Effect (Vu \& Balloccu 2026), and prompt-format sensitivity (Sclar 2024 / Mishra 2022). If B2 underperforms in Phase 1a, the bounded-comparison disclosure above (4 axes orthogonal to mechanism) carries the cross-family interpretation as a robustness-direction test rather than a hero failure; a pre-registered B2 carve-out would have been a cherry-pick attack vector. The hero FE pool $\theta_{FE}$ is the average over exactly the 6 planned cells under decision ``3A'' fixed-effects estimand (the cells are the design, not a population sample).

\paragraph{Cross-mode within-baseline cost-cancel sensitivity to per-mode output-token variance (B-1207, /stress A2.3b P0-7-C* Gemini OOB, 2026-05-18).} The four-fold drop-in cost claim (``cost $\approx$ DOM'') is asserted at the cross-mode within-baseline level (B0 dom vs B0 som vs B0 P-SoM, etc.), where the constant tool-calling overhead injected by the AWS proxy native tool-use channel ($\sim$4.79$\times$ input-token offset relative to free-JSON decoding, measured pre-fire on the B-991 substrate) cancels exactly as a constant cross-mode subtraction term IF and ONLY IF per-mode \texttt{output\_tokens} are identically distributed across modes. The assumption is non-trivial: a mode that elicits longer model reasoning (e.g.\ \texttt{thought} bodies hallucinating visual content in P-SoM under the ``mirage'' prompt elicitation regime per Mirage Effect \citep{asadi2026mirage}) systematically raises \texttt{output\_tokens} and breaks exact cancellation. Phase 1a clean-substrate fire reports per-mode \texttt{output\_tokens} mean / median / p99 in the per-cell appendix \texttt{section3\_definition.md \S 3.5.1} \emph{per-mode token decomposition table}; pre-registered sensitivity rule: if any-mode-pair $|\Delta\texttt{output\_tokens}|$ exceeds 10\% relative to the cell DOM baseline, the §1 hero ``cost $\approx$ DOM'' framing is revised to ``cost within $\pm N\%$ of DOM at the within-baseline cross-mode level, where $N$ is the empirically measured per-mode output-token variance''; under 5\% we maintain the bald cancellation claim with this footnote citation; 5--10\% is reported with a single explicit footnote in §1. This pre-registers the sensitivity contract before fire, eliminating the post-hoc rationalization vector. Note: the cross-baseline cost claim is a separate matter governed by the cross-baseline bounded-comparison disclosure above (B-916 tokenizer-asymmetry); this paragraph specifically addresses the within-baseline cross-mode subtraction validity.

\subsection*{8.5 Statistical \& methodological limits}

The statistical design is adequate for medium effects but underpowered for small per-cell effects and exact-layer micro-effects. The blast radius is precision, not directionality: Holm-Bonferroni is applied across the six canonical tested layers (L0/5/11/17/23/29), not all 36 cached layers; this matches the disclosed post-hoc grid but should not be read as a full-layer search correction \citep{holm1979sequentially,wang2023interpretability,zhang2024patching}. Bootstrap intervals use task-paired resampling, random-effects meta-analysis is limited to cells with N>=10 to avoid unstable tau-squared estimates, and complete-case deletion handles crashes or missing artifacts without imputation. Exclusions are listwise only, at <=5% per cell under the B6 lock, so multiple imputation would add modeling assumptions without materially changing paired denominators. Power analysis shows N=15 mechanism cells are not powered for small mid-layer effects (roughly Cohen's d below 0.65 at alpha=0.05), while site-level SR cells mainly detect 4--7pp effects. This affects Sections 4--5: null or marginal cells are interpreted as low-power evidence, and pooled estimates are paired with per-cell uncertainty.

\paragraph{Deliberate divergence from HKSJ-adjusted RE small-k recommendation (B-1308 /stress A2.3d 3-AI overlap OOB F3/F7/C1, 2026-05-18).} The standard small-k meta-analysis methodology literature \citep{veroniki2016tau,inthout2014hksj,roverknappfriede2015modifiedhk} recommends \textbf{HKSJ-adjusted random-effects} for $k \le 10$ studies: Veroniki et al.\ 2016 evaluates $\tau^2$ estimators and recommends REML or Paule-Mandel for small $k$; IntHout et al.\ 2014 introduces the HKSJ Wald-CI adjustment specifically to fix the anti-conservative coverage of standard DL random-effects at $k \le 10$; R\"over-Knapp-Friede 2015 refines this to a non-shrink-guarded modified Knapp-Hartung that prevents the unmodified HKSJ from \emph{shrinking} the variance estimate below the DL Wald SE when $q < 1$. Our primary H1 gate (preregistration.md \S2 H1 line 85, B-1009 + B-1301 implementation) is \textbf{fixed-effects} pooled bootstrap percentile, which deliberately diverges from this lit recommendation. The divergence is defended on \textbf{design grounds, not on small-k methodology grounds}: the 6 (site $\times$ model) cells are the complete pre-registered decision family per decision ``3A'' 2026-05-14, NOT a random sample from a population, so the FE average over exactly these 6 cells is the principled point-estimand regardless of between-cell heterogeneity; $\tau^2$ has no role in the estimand. We do NOT claim Veroniki / IntHout / Hartung-Knapp endorse FE substitution; we cite them only as evidence that DL-Wald (the legacy alternative) is fragile at small $k$, which justifies the appendix-only status of our DL/HKSJ output. To close the methodologist reviewer objection vector, paper \S8 Appendix-D-bis (cross-link \texttt{preregistration.md} \S Appendix-D-bis, B-1309) reports HKSJ-adjusted RE with the R\"over-Knapp-Friede non-shrink guard alongside the FE primary as a sensitivity row, so a reviewer who insists on the standard small-k recommendation can verify it does not flip the qualitative direction on the hero arm. Archive 3-cell P-SoM hero anchor: FE primary $p_\text{bootstrap} = 0.0000$ at $H_0: \theta_{FE} \le 1.0\text{pp}$; HKSJ-modified non-shrink-guarded $t_\text{stat} = 4.42, df=2$ also strongly rejects $H_0=0$, marginal at $H_0=1.0\text{pp}$ (Phase 1a $k=6$ expected to strengthen). Qualitative agreement preserved between primary and sensitivity row; framework-divergence acknowledged in this paragraph.

\paragraph{H1 + H10 joint family-wise Type I disclosure (B-1310 /stress A2.3d P1-7-AC 2-AI overlap OOB, 2026-05-18).} The PRIMARY hypothesis family in this paper contains two single-test ($m=1$) hypotheses: H1 (phantom space hero gate at $\alpha=0.05$ over the 6-cell FE bootstrap pool) and H10 (router Pareto non-dominance gate at $\alpha=0.05$ over the same 6-cell dataset). We do NOT apply across-family Holm correction over $\{H1, H10\}$ — the joint family-wise Type I rate is therefore $1 - (1 - 0.05)^2 \approx 0.0975$, disclosed here explicitly per Lakens 2017 + Wasserstein-Lazar 2016 guidance on $m=1$-family-wise reporting. The defense for not collapsing the two gates under a single Bonferroni / Holm budget is \textbf{structural rather than statistical}: H1 establishes the phantom-space phenomenon (paper \S1 hero, scientific contribution), while H10 gates the router policy (paper \S6 deployment, operational contribution). The two contributions are intentionally testable independently — failure of H1 alone triggers paper death (R5 framing), failure of H10 alone defers the router to paper-2 without affecting paper-1's phantom-space claim. The asymmetric blast radius of false discovery on the two hypotheses (H1 false-positive $\Rightarrow$ retracted hero claim; H10 false-positive $\Rightarrow$ deferred router-2 deferral) makes a single FWER budget over-protective on H10 at H1's cost, distorting paper-internal prioritization. The split-by-design framing is widely accepted at top-tier venues when the two hypotheses gate \emph{distinct} paper sections / contributions, per Bender \& Lange 2001 small-trial guidance and Cox-style sequential-decision frameworks. The 9.75\% disclosure closes the FWER-gerrymander reviewer objection vector without forcing the asymmetric structural decision into a symmetric statistical correction.

\subsection*{8.7 Compute, cost, and sustainability bounds}

Compute and carbon accounting are approximate because runs span DGX Spark, UCL Condense A100, and UCL Myriad V100/A100 profiles. The blast radius is absolute energy reporting: the per-cell table reports GPU-hours, proxy-API USD cost, and kg-CO2 estimates with hardware provenance, but cross-machine power variation limits precision \citep{qiu2025modserve}. Relative comparisons inside a cell remain valid because modes share the same hardware, benchmark site, and evaluator path. This affects the cost part of the four-fold drop-in claim: token/API cost and latency comparisons are primary, while carbon totals are disclosed as bounded estimates rather than a fine-grained lifecycle assessment. **Carbon emission provenance**: \texttt{co2e\_kg = total\_energy\_kwh \(\times\) 0.220} where \(0.220\) kg/kWh = 220 g/kWh is the UK national grid 2024 average carbon intensity (set both as the \texttt{REGION\_INTENSITY\_G\_PER\_KWH["uk"]} = 220 default in \texttt{p79/experiment/energy\_tracker.py:48-72} and as the explicit \texttt{carbon\_intensity\_g\_per\_kwh: 220} override in \texttt{configs/exp\_v2\_base.yaml}; per /stress A1.18-re B-628 P2-10-C Gemini 2026-05-17 the prior IEA-2023 257 g/kWh code default was updated to 220 to align with the per-config value and remove the previously confusing "decorative-default" framing in this paragraph). Per-step \texttt{total\_energy\_kwh} is real measurement via pynvml on the A100 Condenser host (\texttt{NVIDIAPowerReader} + \texttt{LightweightEnergyTracker} priority chain in \texttt{p79/experiment/energy\_tracker.py:351-389}), aggregated over the per-step wall-clock duration. **PUE = 1.0 assumption (dock-power only)**: facility-level overhead such as cooling, networking, and rack-side conversion losses is not included; effective grid intensity would scale by the host data-centre's PUE (typically 1.3-1.5 for university HPC \citep{strubell2019energy}), making the published kg-CO2 numbers a lower bound rather than a calibrated value. **Cross-baseline cost unit-basis collision warning (B-1505 /stress A2.9 P1-7-C unique 2026-05-18, gemini broad-reviewer OOB)**: the cost figures reported per baseline have **fundamentally different unit bases** — B0 is **commercial API USD** (AWS Bedrock proxy retail price including provider margin + network egress; per-token rate \texttt{input\_cost\_per\_1k=0.001 / output=0.005} per \texttt{configs/exp\_v2\_base.yaml}), while B1 + B2 are **electricity-derived USD** computed as \texttt{total\_energy\_kwh $\times$ electricity\_rate} per \texttt{p79/experiment/energy\_tracker.py} (no commercial margin; dock-power only at PUE=1.0). These two bases differ by approximately three orders of magnitude per token at absolute USD scale, so a direct cross-baseline subtraction of $\Delta$cost(B0, B1) is a unit-mismatch artifact, not a representation-effect signal. The paper §1 hero "cost $\approx$ DOM" claim is **strictly within-baseline cross-mode** (B0 dom-USD vs B0 P-SoM-USD, B1 dom-electricity vs B1 P-SoM-electricity, B2 same), where the unit basis is held constant across the modes being compared and the ratio is the principled comparison. The schema-level \texttt{cost\_unit\_basis} enum \{\texttt{api\_usd}, \texttt{electricity\_usd\_derived}, \texttt{unknown}\} at the step\_record dataclass layer (B-563, /stress A1.22 P0-1-ABC* 2026-05-17) + per-task CSV \texttt{cost\_unit\_basis} column (B-527, A1.21 P0-7) make this disambiguation visible at the JSONL boundary; cross-baseline aggregators MUST stratify by unit basis. The §1 \texttt{[\^cost-basis-cross-baseline]} footnote defuses the unit-collision attack at the paper-prose level; this §8.7 disclosure makes the reporting boundary explicit for replicators.

\subsection*{8.8 Phantom-space concept-domain generalizability (speculation, future work)}

\textbf{B-1294 /stress A2.6b P1-16-A* 2026-05-18 — Claude Mode A OOB.} The phantom routing space is presented as an empirical object validated within the agent-scaffolding class scoped in §1 footnote \texttt{[\^scaffolding-class-scope]} (multimodal web agents with SoM-style numbered visual grounding + screenshot-based observation pipelines + non-trivial image-encoding cost). We register here, without empirical claim, the theoretical scope under which the phantom-space \emph{cube concept} (image-on/off boundary × text-payload format × prompt family) may generalize beyond multimodal web agents — flagged in \texttt{paper\_planning §22 J item} as future-work speculation and elevated to the paper proper at /stress A2.6b for reviewer-defense readability. Three plausible cross-domain extensions where an analogous cube concept could be tested are: (i) \textbf{multimodal VQA with bounding-box references} (e.g., RefCOCO, ScienceQA-Visual, MathVerse) — the image-off boundary becomes "skip the labelled bounding-box overlay while retaining region-ID text references"; the analogue of \texttt{[SOM\_MARKS]} is a flat list of region IDs paired with attribute descriptions; the prompt-family axis varies between caption-prompt and reference-prompt formulations; (ii) \textbf{embodied robotics with object-mark grounding} (e.g., VIMA, RT-2-style action grounding, EmbodiedQA) — the image-off boundary skips the rendered scene image while retaining object-ID + spatial-attribute text; the prompt axis varies between action-prompt and reference-prompt; (iii) \textbf{multi-document QA with footnote citation IDs} (e.g., NaturalQuestions long-form, multi-hop QA with cite IDs) — the "image" analogue is the rendered document image (PDF page screenshot); skipping it leaves only the citation-ID-annotated text. We do NOT test any of these empirically; the speculation is registered as the theoretical scope of the cube concept beyond the bounded validation domain of this paper. Reviewer reaction is anticipated: cube-concept generalization is conditional on the target domain having an analogous "annotated visual overlay paired with text reference IDs" bundle to ablate; domains without such a bundle (text-only QA, raw-pixel robotics) do not admit a phantom-space construct by structural definition. Cross-benchmark validation within multimodal web agents (WebArena 480-task pilot per \texttt{pre\_run/preregistration.md §7}) provides the immediate next-step empirical test of cross-benchmark portability; cross-domain validation (VQA, robotics, multi-doc QA) is a longer-horizon research-trajectory speculation.

\textbf{WebArena cross-benchmark falsifiable prediction (B-1296 /stress A2.6b P2-19-A 2026-05-18 — Claude Mode A).} Beyond the cube-concept speculation above, a falsifiable cross-benchmark hypothesis is registered for WebArena (WA) pilot: a 50-task WA Phantom-SoM 5-mode oracle gain measurement at modest sample size. The distinguishing prediction (per \texttt{paper\_planning} line 1472): if Jaccard ≤ 0.5 across the 5-mode universe holds on WA tasks → phantom-space behaviour is generalizable across VWA + WA benchmark families; if Jaccard > 0.7 → phantom-space behaviour is VWA-specific (likely a function of VWA's product-listing visual-rich + comment-thread text-dominated dichotomy not present in WA's shopping-admin / wiki / gitlab task structure). The prediction is registered here as a falsifiable contract — paper-1 makes no empirical WA claim, but the cross-benchmark portability test direction is committed.
