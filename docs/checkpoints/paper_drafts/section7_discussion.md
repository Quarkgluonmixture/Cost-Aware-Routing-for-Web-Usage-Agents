### 7.1 Representation as a deployable control surface

The central object of this paper turns a normally-fixed engineering choice — how a browser state is serialized for the model — into a control variable the agent system can select per task. Three properties make that control surface *deployable* rather than merely interesting. First, it is **model-fixed**: unlike model routing, the phantom arms require no second model to host, quantize, or keep warm. Second, it is **training-free at inference**: the `[SOM_MARKS]` payload is a regex pass plus a deterministic 1..K renumber over the same accessibility-tree text the DOM agent already consumes (§3.5), so a deployment that can run DOM can run Phantom-SoM by changing what it forwards to the model. Third, it is **cost-equivalent to DOM by construction** and lower-latency than full SoM, because no per-step marked screenshot is encoded (§1, §4.2).

These three are architectural consequences of the image-off boundary, not empirical discoveries. The empirical content sits on top of them: §4.3 shows the arms open **low-overlap success pools**, so the choice of representation is not cosmetic — Phantom-SoM contributes incremental drop-one oracle value (reddit, classifieds) that DOM, full SoM, and Vision do not subsume. §6 then asks the deployment question: can a static per-task classifier realize part of that oracle ceiling at acceptable misrouting regret? The pre-registered §6.4 answer is **site-conditional viability** — hypothesized to hold on visually-heterogeneous classifieds and to collapse toward an always-Phantom-SoM baseline on text-dominated reddit — with the numbers deferred to the Pass-2 fire. The discussion-level takeaway is independent of those numbers: representation is a first-class, cheap, model-internal control surface, distinct in kind from the model-, precision-, and modality-level surfaces the efficiency literature has so far mapped.

### 7.2 Representation routing among efficiency-routing methods

The closest neighbors to this work all route **compute** (full positioning and the routing-axis spectrum table are in §6.8). The model-routing line chooses *which model* answers under a cost-quality tradeoff \citep{chen2023frugalgpt,ong2025routellm,peale2026flexibleRouting,shirkavand2025cscr}; PANDO routes *model capacity* (an expensive planner versus a cheap actor) inside a single VisualWebArena agent \citep{li2026pando}; DMR routes *numeric precision* (a bf16 versus a quantized variant of the same model) per step \citep{li2026dmr}; ReVision routes the *visual-token budget* by dropping temporally-redundant screenshot patches \citep{abaskohi2026revision}; and early-exit halting routes *when to stop* within an episode \citep{lu2025earlyexit}. Each of these holds the observation representation fixed and varies the amount or kind of computation spent on it.

Representation routing is **orthogonal** to all of them: it varies which rendering of the same browser state the fixed model consumes, with compute budget held constant. The practical consequence is that the axes **compose** rather than compete. A production stack could, in principle, route representation (this paper), escalate model on a stalled trajectory (FrugalGPT-style), and prune visual tokens on the images it does send (ReVision-style) at the same time; our contribution is additive to the compute-axis stack, not a substitute for it. We deliberately resist the stronger reading that the phantom space *replaces* model routing. Its lever is the per-step screenshot-encoding cost, which is large for current vision towers but shrinks as cheaper vision encoders arrive; the §1 scaffolding-class footnote registers this as a boundary condition, not a permanent property.

### 7.3 Generalization across sites, model families, and scaffolding classes

The phantom space's *existence* and its *utility* generalize differently, and it is worth separating the two.

**Across sites.** The classifieds-versus-reddit asymmetry (§4.2) and the site-conditional router viability (§6.4) say that where the phantom space *exists* (both sites), its *utility* is site-dependent: full SoM wins on visually-dense marketplace pages, while the image-skipped representation is competitive or better on text-dominated forum threads. At N=2 sites this is a directional pattern, not a correlational claim (§1); Phase 1b shopping is the staged out-of-distribution site test (§8.1), and the WebArena cross-benchmark mini-prereg (§8.8) commits a falsifiable portability contract before any cross-benchmark fire.

**Across model families.** The design verifies two within-family Qwen cells (B0 235B, B1 4B) plus one cross-family Gemma cell (B2 = Gemma-3-4B), under a pre-registered claim-tier gate that downgrades the cross-family claim if B2 fails to replicate rather than masking non-replication inside the pooled estimate (§8.1). Judge Circuits \citep{feldhus2026judgecircuits} sharpens *why* B2 is the fragile corner. On the same backbone families we use (Qwen2.5, Gemma-3, Llama-3), it localizes a sparse, causal sub-computation that maps a continuous judgment signal through fragile, format-specific terminal branches — and crucially, it finds the *separability* of that format-conditional computation from world knowledge to be **family- and scale-dependent**: modular in Qwen2.5-7B/14B and Gemma-3-27B, but **entangled in Gemma-3-12B** (where zero-ablating the latent evaluator collapses MMLU-clinical accuracy from 81% to 19%). Our B2 is smaller still, so the format/content separability the phantom axis assumes may be weaker for B2 than for the Qwen baselines. We register this as a **predicted cross-family confound to watch** when B2 results land, not as a settled mechanism. Two caveats bound the transfer (§2.3): Judge Circuits studies a judge's *output* format, not an agent's *input* observation, and it is text-only with no agent loop — the link is conceptual, not evidential.

**Across scaffolding classes.** The construct presupposes screenshot-based, SoM-style scaffolding with a marked image paired to a textual element legend; text-only and coordinate-only agents fall outside it because there is no annotated image to skip (§1 scaffolding-class footnote, §8.1). The more speculative question — whether a "skip one modality of a redundant bundle" move generalizes beyond web SoM to other multimodal agent settings — is registered as the §8.8 concept-domain speculation and is explicitly not an empirical claim of this paper.

### 7.4 Mechanistic outlook

This paper rests on a **behavioural** account (the two-knob ablation, §4.4): text representation appears to shape exploration, while prompt family appears to modulate commitment timing. It does **not** claim a mechanism; the activation-patching, layer-probe, and logit-lens work that would test the account directly is deferred to a follow-up paper (§8.6). Two external anchors nonetheless make the behavioural account plausible rather than ad hoc. Prompt-format sensitivity \citep{sclar2024promptformat,mishra2022reframing} establishes that semantically-equivalent surface forms move model behaviour, which is the precondition for any representation-routing effect. Judge Circuits \citep{feldhus2026judgecircuits} goes further, localizing a *causal* format-conditional sub-computation on the same backbone families — evidence that "format" carries separable computation rather than only correlating with output variance. Neither is web-agent evidence, and we are careful not to import either as a mechanism claim of our own; their role here is to motivate the follow-up paper's hypotheses and to predict (via the Gemma-3 entanglement result) which corner of the design is most at risk.

### 7.5 Deployment implications

The deployable form of the phantom space is a **cost-aware, site-aware representation router**. The recommended default is the cheap text rendering, with escalation to full SoM where visual grounding is likely to matter — and, because the routing viability is site-conditional (§6.4), the escalation policy should itself be site-aware rather than uniform. This composes cleanly with the compute-axis methods of §7.2: representation routing reduces the marginal per-step cost *before* any model- or precision-escalation decision is taken, so it is the natural first stage of a layered cost-aware web-agent stack. The bounds within which this claim holds — site count, model-family coverage, scaffolding class, and the evaluator and sequential-protocol estimand caveats — are recorded in §8.
