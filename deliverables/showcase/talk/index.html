① How do the views behave · 6 views · 8 settings · 8,934 task attempts

With only the screenshot, the agent scrolls far more and types far less.

Medians over 8 settings: LOOK scrolls in about 30% of its steps, every other view about 6%; it types about half as often

② How do they fail · tasks only one side solved

Text-only views fail in ways you can name; views with the screenshot just never get there.

Each side vs its own failures elsewhere · text-only: several over 2× · LOOK and BOTH: none stands out · six VisualWebArena settings

③ Does choosing pay

Hindsight says choosing pays; a rerun says how much of that is noise.

Perfect hindsight: +3.45 to +16.35 in 100 · in the one setting rerun for every view, one more view bought +7.14, one rerun +4.46 to +7.59

④ Can it be learned · five learned choices · nested cross-validation · vs always-cheapest

Learned routers buy success only by spending more.

Win region = cheaper and no worse. Learned: 0 of 8. Perfect hindsight: 1 of 8.
