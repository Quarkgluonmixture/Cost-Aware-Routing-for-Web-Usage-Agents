\caption{What a cost number contains. Every cost figure elsewhere is \texttt{cost/ep}, a single number; the schema splits it additively into what bought a canonical action and what was burned re-parsing the model's output. Summed over each backbone's conditions, protocol repair is 0.53\% of B0's bill and 4.38\% of B2's: a 8.2× spread across model families for the same task set, and the worst per-episode parse-error wait counts all sit on B2. This is small in absolute terms and is not a correction to any reported cost. It bounds something else: a cross-family cost comparison is not quite comparing like with like, because one family pays a protocol tax the others do not. Source: \texttt{results/phantom\_paper/cross\_site/cross\_site\_aggregation.csv} (via \texttt{aggregate\_cross\_site.py}).}
