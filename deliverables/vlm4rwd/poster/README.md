# VLM4RWD 2026 poster

`VLM4RWD2026_Paper18_Poster_61x85cm.pdf` is the print file: 61 cm × 85 cm portrait, the board size in the
organisers' 2026-10-19 poster email (one Command strip per corner; take it down right after the session).

It is the REALM @ EMNLP 2026 poster (`REALM2026_Paper192_Poster_A0_source.pdf`, A0, as submitted to
Underline on 2026-10-07; copied from the Windows machine's Downloads, SHA-256 `5a01502b…`) with only the
venue line changed, regenerated by:

```bash
python make_poster.py REALM2026_Paper192_Poster_A0_source.pdf VLM4RWD2026_Paper18_Poster_61x85cm.pdf
```

(needs `pypdf` and `reportlab`). The script deletes the two REALM venue lines from the content stream,
paints their area in the footer colour and sets "VLM4RWD @ NeurIPS 2026 · Non-archival poster · Paper 18 /
11 December 2026 · Sydney". Scaling is uniform, so ~0.44 cm of white remains at each side.

The poster body is unchanged from REALM. It does not carry the camera-ready additions (the success–cost
frontier, the per-cell rerun table), and its "reruns flip 5–14%, so oracle ceilings are partly noise" line
is the hosted-backbone statement, which the camera-ready keeps.
