\section{Other routing constructions}\label{app:otherrouters}

Before settling on the two routers of \S\ref{sec:routing}, we tried the constructions in Table~\ref{tab:otherrouters}. They were exploratory: each was run offline on recorded runs, mostly with all six modes as the choice set, and none is corrected for multiple comparisons or carried into the main analysis. None selects modes better than the best fixed mode. The richer learners and the related-work adaptations fail on the same cell where the main routers fail, so the negative result of \S\ref{sec:routing} is not specific to logistic regression on 18 features.
