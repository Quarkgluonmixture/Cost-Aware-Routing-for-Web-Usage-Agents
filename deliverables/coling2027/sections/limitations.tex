\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Coverage of reruns.} The preference test and decision study (\S\ref{sec:preference}) rest on the only three cells with every mode run twice, two of which share a backbone. Of the remaining eight of the eleven cells, five have some modes rerun and three have none. On classifieds, B2 solves about 2\% of tasks, so its two reruns cannot bound an effect.

\paragraph{Breadth.} VWA-reddit and WA-reddit are task sets on the same application, so the study covers three applications, not four benchmarks. GPT-5.6 was run on classifieds only, and its Vision run is excluded for a coordinate-contract defect. A parser defect also let GPT-5.6 take actions beyond the 30-step budget in every mode (Appendix~\ref{app:harness}). The ex-ante rule of \S\ref{sec:screenshot} works on classifieds only.

\paragraph{Shopping filtering.} The clean shopping cells drop every task on which the search-box defect fired in any mode, as well as the 42 grid-order-sensitive and 4 wishlist tasks (Appendix~\ref{app:harness}). Whether the defect fired depends on what the agent typed, so the filter selects tasks by behaviour under the conditions being compared. The clean cells estimate the comparison on an observed defect-free subset, not on the benchmark, and B0 and B1 keep different subsets (368 and 192 tasks). Results on all tasks are reported alongside (Tables~\ref{tab:routing} and \ref{tab:cells}); no conclusion rests on the filtered cells alone.

\paragraph{What the routing result does not show.} The routers use 18 pre-run features and logistic models; richer features, language-model routers or multiple runs per training task may do better. With single-run labels and these features, the routers do not reliably beat the fixed choices and their mixtures. The result does not show that per-task routing is impossible. Step-level switching is out of scope.

\paragraph{Inherited state.} Reddit and shopping are reset before each run but not between tasks, so an episode can be credited for state an earlier episode created. A mechanical audit removes reddit successes credited without visiting the target forum and changes one comparison in the paper (\S\ref{sec:deploy}). The audit cannot see an episode that visits an already-subscribed forum and leaves without acting, so reddit outcomes may still carry inherited credit, and reruns may reproduce it.

\paragraph{Exploratory analyses.} The preference test, the reading of label changes in \S\ref{sec:preference}, the frontier comparisons and the deployable-router estimates were designed after the runs were collected. Only the preference test is corrected across cells (Holm). The frontier $p$-values account for choosing each curve's best point but are not corrected across curves. Only the pooled three-mode triage curve survives Holm correction across the 14 curve maxima of Table~\ref{tab:routing}. The bootstrap intervals for policies with a selected threshold are read as magnitudes, not tests.

\paragraph{Cost.} API charges and local-serving estimates are different quantities and are never pooled. Local cost also depends on the pricing basis: the pooled three-mode triage curve clears its null under billed cost but not under wall-clock time or under GPU time on the locally served cells (\S\ref{sec:routing}).

\section*{Ethics statement}

All episodes ran against self-hosted copies of the VisualWebArena and WebArena sites, and no human subjects were involved. No real account or person was contacted, but agents could follow external links off the self-hosted sites. In the audited runs, 3--6\% of reddit episodes and at most 0.3\% of classifieds episodes visited live public pages. Sites were reset before each run. The API-served backbones were used under their providers' terms. Agents that act on websites can cause harm when deployed against live services; this study measures observation choices on self-hosted sites and releases no agent that acts outside them.

\paragraph{Availability.} The anonymous supplementary material contains the agent and run configurations, per-episode outcomes and costs for every run used, the analysis scripts, and every analysis product cited in the text. A guide in the supplement maps each number in the paper to the product field it comes from. One script regenerates the figures and tables from the products. Full trajectories (screenshots and page text) are too large for the supplement and will be released with the camera-ready version.
