\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{How web agents observe the page.} Agents have read HTML or accessibility trees \citep{deng2023mind2web,agentoccam2025}, screenshots grounded to elements \citep{zheng2024seeact,he2024webvoyager,zheng2024uground}, or set-of-marks overlays that fuse the two \citep{yang2023som,koh2024visualwebarena}. A parallel line shrinks or reshapes the text observation \citep{kerboua2025focusagent,schiepanski2025d2snap,enomoto2026observation}, and some systems combine several grounding routes \citep{li2026avenirweb}. OSWorld compares screenshot, accessibility-tree, combined and set-of-marks inputs across models and finds the best input model-dependent \citep{xie2024osworld}, and \citet{chi2024elementordering} compare screenshot-plus-text with text only on a VisualWebArena subset. Closer to adaptivity, \citet{li2026aoi} gate visual observation components during computer-use tasks, and \citet{liu2026adaptivevlm} route each action of a computer-use agent among vision-language models. Neither chooses between text and screenshot per task before it runs. The works above compare observation designs by benchmark score or adapt them online. OSWorld already shows that the best observation varies. We contribute a matched removal of the screenshot, repeated task-level outcomes, a test of task structure beyond difficulty, and a deployable per-task choice judged against mixtures of the fixed choices.

\paragraph{Routing and cascades.} Choosing among models per query to trade quality for cost is well studied for language models \citep{chen2023frugalgpt,ong2025routellm,ding2024hybridllm,gupta2024cascades,peale2026flexibleRouting}; see \citet{moslem2026routingsurvey} for a survey. For web agents, \citet{webrouter2025} learn query-specific routing for cost-sensitive agents, and \citet{hajimiri2026budgetmatched} judge agent components at a matched budget. Early exit \citep{lu2025earlyexit} stops agents that are unlikely to succeed, a decision about difficulty. In our data, such decisions are the only part of routing whose pooled gain over the frontier clears its null in hindsight. Routing benchmarks and method papers compare routers against random mixtures of fixed models \citep{hu2024routerbench,jitkrittum2025uniroute}. Under unified conditions, several learned routers fail to beat a simple baseline \citep{li2026llmrouterbench}. \citet{lee2026routinggap} finds that assigning one model per task type in advance recovers most of the oracle routing gain on seven task types in three languages, and that reruns change the score of 5\% of model--question pairs. Item-response routers estimate query difficulty and model ability \citep{song2025irtrouter}. We route over observation representations rather than models, take the mixture baseline from this literature, and add what it rarely has: reruns of identical agent conditions and a test of per-task structure beyond difficulty.

\paragraph{Reliability of agent evaluation.} Repeated trials of the same agent disagree, which $\tau$-bench measures with pass$^k$ \citep{yao2025taubench}. Inference itself is not deterministic even at fixed settings \citep{he2025nondeterminism,yuan2025numerical}, prompts change outcomes in ways unrelated to content \citep{sclar2024promptformat}, and agent benchmarks carry evaluator and task defects \citep{elhattami2025webarenaverified,bhat2026benchmarkingbenchmarks}. Our reruns measure run-to-run disagreement for web agents at the task level and show its effect on per-task routing labels. Appendix~\ref{app:harness} lists the harness defects we found.
