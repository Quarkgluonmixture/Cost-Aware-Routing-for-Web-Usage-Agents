\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{How web agents observe the page.} Agents have read HTML or accessibility trees \citep{deng2023mind2web,agentoccam2025}, screenshots grounded to elements \citep{zheng2024seeact,he2024webvoyager,zheng2024uground}, or set-of-marks overlays that fuse the two \citep{yang2023som,koh2024visualwebarena}. A parallel line shrinks or reshapes the text observation \citep{kerboua2025focusagent,schiepanski2025d2snap,enomoto2026observation}, and some systems combine several grounding routes \citep{li2026avenirweb}. These works compare observation designs at the level of a benchmark score; we ask whether the choice should be made per task, and isolate the screenshot with a matched ablation rather than comparing whole pipelines.

\paragraph{Routing and cascades.} Choosing among models per query to trade quality for cost is well studied for language models \citep{chen2023frugalgpt,ong2025routellm,ding2024hybridllm,gupta2024cascades,peale2026flexibleRouting}; see \citet{moslem2026routingsurvey} for a survey. For web agents, \citet{webrouter2025} learn query-specific routing for cost-sensitive agents, and \citet{hajimiri2026budgetmatched} judge agent components at a matched budget. Early exit \citep{lu2025earlyexit} stops agents that are unlikely to succeed --- the difficulty lever that, in our data, is the only part of routing that leaves room above the fixed frontier. We route over observation representations rather than models, and add two controls these settings rarely have: reruns of identical conditions, and random mixtures of fixed choices as the baseline.

\paragraph{Reliability of agent evaluation.} Inference is not deterministic even at fixed settings \citep{he2025nondeterminism,yuan2025numerical}, prompts change outcomes in ways unrelated to content \citep{sclar2024promptformat}, and agent benchmarks carry evaluator and task defects \citep{elhattami2025webarenaverified,bhat2026benchmarkingbenchmarks}. Our reruns quantify the first for web agents at the task level and show what it does to per-task routing labels; the harness defects we found are listed in Appendix~\ref{app:harness}.
