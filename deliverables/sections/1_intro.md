Beats to cover, in the order the body follows:

1. Web agents must be given the page in *some* representation, and the field has
   settled on three shapes without treating the choice as a variable.

   of variation produces that difference.
3. If failures differ, a per-task choice has value. We bound it.
4. The bound is not what it looks like: the same increment is bought by rerunning
   one arm.
5. Five routing policies, none of which beats a fixed single mode.
6. The gap between the ceiling and the policies is the contribution --- it is a
   supervision problem, not a tuning problem.
