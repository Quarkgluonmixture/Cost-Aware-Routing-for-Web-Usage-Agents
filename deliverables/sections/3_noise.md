# What a rerun buys

\todo{Prose. This section exists to make the next one honest and should read as a
measurement, not as a caveat.}

**The claim.** Before any statement about what a second representation buys, the
same question has to be asked of the representation already in hand: what does
running it again buy?

Adding the single best distinct arm buys 1.97--7.14pp of oracle coverage. Rerunning
one arm, unchanged, buys 2.0--7.6pp on the cells where a replicate exists. On
`cls`$\cdot$`B0` the two are 7.14pp and 7.59pp: **the rerun is worth slightly more
than the new representation.**

Two limits belong on that sentence. Only two cells carry a measured replicate at
all, and the draws are two per cell rather than a distribution --- the
exchangeability null behind them puts one standard deviation at 2.32--2.53pp, so
the level a single rerun is unlikely to manufacture is nearer 3.8--4.2pp than the
band's own 2.23pp upper edge. The union of *five* reruns, which is the honest
comparator for a six-arm oracle, has never been measured.
